Top Win — landing previews

Test builds of the two campaign landings, rebuilt on tw-lp-template. Nothing here is live traffic, and neither production URL is affected by anything on this page.

  • tw-penalty Pick a corner, the keeper saves the first, the second scores. Retrofit onto the template.
  • tw-flip-cards-lp Turn three cards, take the bonus. One HTML file now, not three.
The registration links (Terms, Privacy, Log in, Go to website) are deliberately unset in both builds, so they are not links at all until the real URLs arrive. The form posts nowhere: it validates, logs its payload to the console and walks the confirmation screen.
